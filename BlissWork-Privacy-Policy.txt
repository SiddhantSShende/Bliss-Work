BLISSWORK PRIVACY POLICY

Version 1.0  |  Last updated: 3 July 2026

BlissWork is offered internationally. This Privacy Policy applies to all users
of the Service worldwide, regardless of the country from which you access it,
and explains how AEIV Global Private Limited, a company incorporated in India
("AEIV Global", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects
your personal information when you use BlissWork -- our team collaboration
platform, including its web application and desktop application distributed
through the Microsoft Store (the "Service").

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the practices
described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHO WE ARE AND OUR ROLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global is the operator of BlissWork. For personal information relating to
your account registration, billing, and communications with us, AEIV Global
acts as the data controller (or "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal
Data Protection Act, 2023).

For content you and your colleagues create inside a workspace (such as
messages), the organisation that owns the workspace is the controller, and AEIV
Global acts as a data processor on that organisation's behalf, processing such
data only per our agreement with them. Business customers may request a Data
Processing Addendum (DPA) by contacting us.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

We collect only the information needed to provide a secure collaboration
service:

a) Account and profile information
   - Your name and display name
   - Username and email address
   - A securely hashed version of your password (using the Argon2id algorithm
     -- we never store or have access to your plaintext password)

b) Organisation information
   - Organisation name and identifier
   - Your role within the organisation (e.g. administrator or member) and your
     workspace memberships

c) Invitations
   - When you invite a colleague, we process the invitee's email address and
     assigned role to deliver the invitation. You confirm that you have a
     proper basis to share that person's email address with us.

d) Content and activity data
   - Messages you send, conversation membership, and read receipts, so your
     team's conversations synchronise across devices

e) Presence data
   - Real-time online/connection status, so colleagues can see who is currently
     active

f) Authentication and session data
   - A single essential authentication cookie named "refresh_token", set as HttpOnly,
     Secure, and SameSite, used solely to keep you signed in. We do NOT use
     advertising, marketing, or third-party analytics cookies.
   - Short-lived access tokens used to authenticate your requests

g) Security and audit logs
   - Records of account and administrative actions (who did what, and when),
     maintained in a tamper-evident form for security and accountability

h) Technical and log data
   - Standard server logs including IP address, browser/device type, and
     timestamps, generated automatically when you use the Service and used for
     security, diagnostics, and reliability

i) Payment information (for paid subscriptions)
   - See Section 6. We do not store your full payment card details on our
     servers.

j) Support communications
   - Any information you provide when you contact us for support

We do NOT collect special categories of sensitive data, and we do not require
any information beyond what is necessary to operate the Service.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION AND LEGAL BASES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use your information to:
   - Provide, maintain, and secure the Service (authenticating you, delivering
     messages and invitations, showing presence, syncing your data)
   - Maintain audit trails and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access
   - Provide customer support and respond to your requests
   - Send essential service communications (e.g. security, account, and
     transactional notices)
   - Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms
   - Process payments and manage subscriptions (where applicable)
   - Improve the reliability and performance of the Service

Where the GDPR / UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a
contract (to provide the Service), legitimate interests (to secure and improve
the Service and prevent abuse), consent (where required, which you may withdraw
at any time), and compliance with a legal obligation. Under India's DPDP Act,
we process personal data based on your consent or for legitimate uses as
permitted by law.

We do NOT sell your personal information, and we do NOT use it for advertising
or cross-context behavioural profiling.

We do NOT use your messages, files, or other business content to train
artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. If we introduce AI-powered
features in the future, we will update this Policy and, where required, obtain
your consent before doing so.

4. COOKIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

BlissWork uses a single essential authentication cookie ("refresh_token") to
keep you securely signed in. Because it is strictly necessary to the Service, it

advertising, or social-media tracking cookies.

5. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

We share personal information only in these limited circumstances:

   - Within your organisation: your display name, presence, messages, and
     activity are visible to other members of your workspace, as inherent to a
     collaboration tool.

   - Service providers (processors): we use trusted third parties who process
     data on our behalf under contract, including a transactional email
     provider (to deliver invitation and account emails), our cloud
     hosting/infrastructure provider, and -- for paid plans -- a payment
     processor. These providers are bound to use your data only as we instruct.

   - Distribution platform: the desktop app is distributed via the Microsoft
     Store; Microsoft's own privacy terms govern any data it collects through
     the store.

   - Legal and safety: where required by law, court order, or to protect the
     rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or AEIV Global.

   - Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of
     assets, subject to this Policy.

We do NOT sell or rent your personal information to data brokers, advertisers,
or any third party for their own marketing.

Law enforcement and legal requests: we do not voluntarily hand your data to
governments. We disclose personal information to law enforcement or other
authorities only when compelled by valid legal process (such as a binding court
order or subpoena), or where strictly necessary to protect the rights, safety,
and security of our users, the public, or AEIV Global. Where we are legally
permitted to do so, we will notify the affected organisation before disclosing.

8. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

We retain your personal information for as long as your account or organisation
remains active, and thereafter only as long as necessary to comply with our
legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When an
organisation is deleted, its encryption keys are destroyed (crypto-shredding),
rendering its data permanently unreadable. Residual copies in encrypted backups
are purged on our routine backup-rotation cycle.

How to delete your data:
   - Delete your account: you may delete your individual account at any time
     from your account settings, or by contacting us. This removes your profile
     and personal details from our active systems.
   - Delete a workspace/organisation: an organisation administrator may delete
     the entire organisation. This permanently destroys the organisation's
     encryption keys (crypto-shredding), so its messages and data can no longer
     be read by anyone, including us.
   - Timing: deletions take effect on our active systems promptly, and the
     corresponding data is then removed from encrypted backups as those backups
     expire on our routine rotation cycle. We retain limited records only where
     required by law (for example, tax or accounting records).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR DATA
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Security is core to BlissWork:

   - Encryption at rest: sensitive fields (including email addresses, display
     names, and message content) are encrypted using AES-256-GCM, with
     encryption scoped per organisation.

   - Encryption in transit: all data is transmitted over TLS.

   - Strict tenant isolation: each organisation's data is isolated at the
     database level (row-level security), so one organisation cannot access
     another's data.

   - Password protection: passwords are hashed with Argon2id and are never
     stored in plaintext.

   - Tamper-evident audit logs and crypto-shredding for verifiable, permanent
     deletion.

No system is perfectly secure, but we maintain administrative, technical, and
organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

Breach notification: if we become aware of a security incident that compromises
your personal information, we will notify the affected organisations and users,
and the relevant supervisory authorities, without undue delay and as required
by applicable law (including the GDPR and India's DPDP Act, 2023).

Reporting a vulnerability: we welcome responsible disclosure. If you believe you
have found a security issue, please email siddhant.shende@consultrnr.com. We
will acknowledge your report and work with you to resolve it, and we ask that
you give us a reasonable opportunity to fix the issue before disclosing it
publicly.
