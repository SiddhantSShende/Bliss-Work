BLISSWORK DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (DPA)

Version 1.0  |  Last updated: 3 July 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of, and is subject to, the
BlissWork Terms of Service (the "Agreement") between AEIV Global Private Limited
("AEIV Global", "we", "us", the "Processor") and the customer organisation that
has entered into the Agreement ("Customer", "you", the "Controller"). It applies
to the extent AEIV Global processes Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in
providing BlissWork (the "Service"), and reflects the parties' agreement on such
processing under applicable data protection laws, including the EU and UK GDPR
and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("Applicable Data
Protection Law").

If you are a business customer and require a countersigned copy, contact us at
siddhant.shende@consultrnr.com.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. DEFINITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement.
"Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject", "Personal Data", "Processing", and
"Personal Data Breach" have the meanings given in Applicable Data Protection
Law. "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by AEIV Global to process
Personal Data. "Customer Personal Data" means Personal Data contained in Your
Content that AEIV Global processes on the Customer's behalf.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ROLES AND SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

For Customer Personal Data, the Customer is the Controller and AEIV Global is
the Processor. Where the Customer is itself a processor for another controller,
AEIV Global acts as a sub-processor. Each party will comply with its obligations
under Applicable Data Protection Law. This DPA does not apply to data for which
AEIV Global is an independent controller (such as account-registration and
billing data), which is governed by our Privacy Policy.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PROCESSING ON DOCUMENTED INSTRUCTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global will process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's
documented instructions -- including as set out in this DPA, the Agreement, and
the Customer's configuration and use of the Service -- and as required by law
(in which case we will inform the Customer unless legally prohibited). The
subject matter, nature, purpose, duration, types of Personal Data, and
categories of Data Subjects are described in Annex I. The Customer is
responsible for ensuring it has a lawful basis to provide the Customer Personal
Data and for the accuracy of its instructions.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONFIDENTIALITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global ensures that personnel authorised to process Customer Personal Data
are bound by appropriate obligations of confidentiality and access it only on a
need-to-know basis.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global implements and maintains appropriate technical and organisational
measures to protect Customer Personal Data against accidental or unlawful
destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access, as described
in Annex II. AEIV Global may update these measures provided the level of
protection is not materially reduced.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SUB-PROCESSORS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Customer grants AEIV Global general authorisation to engage Sub-processors
to provide the Service. A current list of Sub-processors is set out in Annex
III. AEIV Global imposes data protection obligations on each Sub-processor that
are no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains responsible for their
performance. We will give the Customer reasonable prior notice of the addition
or replacement of a Sub-processor and, where the Customer has a reasonable,
data-protection-based objection, the parties will work in good faith to resolve
it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Taking into account the nature of the Processing, AEIV Global will assist the
Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as
possible, to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights. If
AEIV Global receives such a request directly, it will, where permitted, direct
the Data Subject to the Customer.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PERSONAL DATA BREACH
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of
a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and will provide
information reasonably available to assist the Customer in meeting its
breach-notification and cooperation obligations.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. IMPACT ASSESSMENTS AND CONSULTATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global will provide reasonable assistance to the Customer with data
protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory
authorities, taking into account the nature of the Processing and the
information available to AEIV Global.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global and its Sub-processors may process Customer Personal Data in
countries other than the Customer's own. Where such transfers are subject to
Applicable Data Protection Law, the parties rely on an appropriate transfer
mechanism -- such as the Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for the UK, the UK
International Data Transfer Addendum) or an adequacy decision -- which are
incorporated by reference where applicable.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. DELETION AND RETURN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

On termination of the Agreement, or on the Customer's request, AEIV Global will
delete or return Customer Personal Data and delete existing copies, except to
the extent retention is required by law. Deletion of an Organisation triggers
crypto-shredding of its encryption keys, rendering its data permanently
unreadable; residual backup copies are purged on our routine rotation cycle.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. AUDITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

AEIV Global will make available to the Customer information reasonably necessary
to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and will allow for and contribute to
audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or an auditor it
mandates, subject to reasonable notice, confidentiality, frequency, and security
requirements. Where practicable, AEIV Global may satisfy audit requests by
providing relevant documentation or third-party reports.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. LIABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each party's liability under or in connection with this DPA is subject to the
limitations and exclusions of liability set out in the Agreement.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TERM
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This DPA takes effect when the Customer accepts the Agreement and continues
until AEIV Global has ceased processing Customer Personal Data and completed
deletion or return under Section 11.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. GOVERNING LAW
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This DPA is governed by the law and subject to the dispute-resolution and
jurisdiction provisions of the Agreement, except where Applicable Data
Protection Law requires otherwise.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Agreement regarding the
processing of Customer Personal Data, this DPA prevails. In the event of a
conflict between this DPA and the Standard Contractual Clauses, the Standard
Contractual Clauses prevail.


========================================================================
ANNEX I -- DETAILS OF PROCESSING
========================================================================

   - Subject matter: AEIV Global's provision of the BlissWork Service to the
     Customer.
   - Duration: the term of the Agreement and until deletion or return of
     Customer Personal Data.
   - Nature and purpose: hosting, storage, transmission, encryption, and display
     of Customer Personal Data to provide team-collaboration features (channels,
     messaging, presence, invitations) and to secure and support the Service.
   - Types of Personal Data: names and display names, usernames, email
     addresses, message and file content, presence and activity data, and
     related metadata submitted through the Service.
   - Categories of Data Subjects: the Customer's authorised users and
     administrators, and the people they invite or communicate with.


========================================================================
ANNEX II -- TECHNICAL AND ORGANISATIONAL MEASURES
========================================================================

   - Encryption at rest: sensitive fields (email addresses, display names,
     message content) encrypted with AES-256-GCM, keyed per organisation.
   - Encryption in transit: TLS for all client and inter-service traffic;
     verified TLS for database connections.
   - Password protection: Argon2id hashing; plaintext passwords are never
     stored.
   - Tenant isolation: database row-level security isolating each organisation's
     data.
   - Access control: least-privilege, authenticated internal service access;
     limited and logged production access.
   - Auditability: tamper-evident, hash-chained audit logs of account and
     administrative actions.
   - Deletion: crypto-shredding of encryption keys on organisation deletion, and
     routine purging of backups.
   - Resilience: encrypted backups and continuous monitoring of the platform.


========================================================================
ANNEX III -- SUB-PROCESSORS
========================================================================

   - Cloud hosting / infrastructure provider -- hosting and storage of Service
     data.
   - Transactional email provider -- delivery of invitation and account emails.
   - Payment processor (for paid plans) -- processing of subscription payments.

A current, more detailed list is available from AEIV Global on request.
