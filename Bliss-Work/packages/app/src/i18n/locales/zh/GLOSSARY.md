# Simplified Chinese (zh) glossary

Simplified Chinese (Mainland China). Informal-neutral register: 「你」 used consistently where a pronoun is needed (「您」 never), otherwise omitted. Chinese has a single plural category, so plural groups contain only `_other`. Full-width punctuation (，。：；！？（）) in prose; ASCII is kept inside placeholders, URLs, code-like tokens and where the English uses `...`/`…`. No spaces between Chinese and Latin letters/digits, except around `{{placeholders}}` and numbers followed by a unit word where it aids legibility.
