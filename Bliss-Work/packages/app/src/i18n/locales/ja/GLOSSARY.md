# Japanese (ja) glossary

Japanese (Japan), polite です・ます体 for sentences; buttons and menu items as concise noun/verb forms. Plural groups use only `_other` (Japanese has one CLDR category). Full-width 、。()?: are used in prose; ASCII is kept inside placeholders, URLs and code-like tokens. No spaces between Japanese and Latin/digits.
