# Hindi (hi) glossary

Devanagari Hindi, respectful "आप" register, ASCII digits, nuktas used (क़ ख़ ग़ ज़ ड़ ढ़ फ़). Widely understood English loanwords are written in Devanagari (टास्क, चैनल, मीटिंग); natural Hindi elsewhere. Plural groups use `_one` / `_other` (CLDR: 0 and 1 are "one"). Hindi is SOV, so strings with `{{name}}` keep the name before a postposition (को, का, ने, से) and the verb last. Brand and third-party names stay Latin.
